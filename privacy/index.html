Gridrun Privacy Policy

Effective for Gridrun 1.0, updated 5 October 2026

Gridrun is a block puzzle game that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no account, advertising, purchases, developer analytics, tracking, or developer-operated backend, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data collected

None. Gridrun does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data.

Local data

Gridrun stores your settings, your stats (such as your best score and recent scores) and your game in progress on your device, so you can continue a game and see your progress. This information never leaves your iPhone.

Deleting data

You can reset your stats in Gridrun's Settings. Deleting Gridrun removes all of its locally stored data.

Children

Gridrun is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Gridrun's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is distributed.

Contact
